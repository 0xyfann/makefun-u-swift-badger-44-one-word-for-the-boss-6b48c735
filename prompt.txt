Write a Python (stdlib only) program, main.py, for the make.fun
competition "One Word for the Boss" (u-swift-badger-44-one-word-for-the-boss-6b48c735).

The program must write exactly ONE word describing a boss to
/output/entry.txt, honoring the MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR environment variable.

Rules from RULES.txt:
- The file must be UTF-8 text of 1 to 30 characters, not only whitespace.
- Exactly one word: no spaces, no phrase, no caption, no explanation.
- The judge (a comedy editor) weighs, in order: (1) it is a single word
  a person would actually use about their boss, (2) funny on its own,
  (3) rings true about a real boss habit, (4) fresh — not the first word
  anyone would reach for, (5) travels across industries.

Pick an original word that is funny, true, fresh, and universal.
Avoid the obvious ("micromanager", "bossy", "strict") — they lose on
freshness. The word I chose: "Unfireable" — the boss who can never be
fired, no matter what. It is a single word, lands without a caption,
is true of bosses everywhere, and is fresher than the usual answers.

The program should validate the word against the gate (1–30 chars,
single token, no surrounding whitespace) before writing it, with no
trailing newline.
